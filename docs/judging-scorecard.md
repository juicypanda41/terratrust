## Minimum evidence package to produce

- `README.md`: concise problem, solution, users, setup/use, architecture or operating model, SDG targets, metrics, scalability, team, attributions, and links.
- Research appendix: source table with claim, source, date, relevance, and limitations.
- Validation record: interview/survey script, anonymized results, usability tests, or benchmark protocol and raw outputs.
- Metrics table: baseline, target, method, sample size, current result, caveat, and owner for at least three impact/performance metrics.
- Architecture diagram or Business Model Canvas, with design rationale.
- Phased scale plan with milestones, assumptions, projected reach, costs/resources, and risks.
- Pitch deck and timed 2-3 minute script/storyboard.
- `docs/ai-use-log.md` completed with honest, detailed disclosure.
- Final link-check and demo rehearsal record.
