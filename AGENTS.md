# OurPlanet.Rocks Hackathon Project Rules

This workspace is for the OurPlanet.Rocks 2026 hackathon. Every implementation, design, research, testing, documentation, and presentation decision must support a complete rubric submission.

## North star

Target 100% of the applicable rubric: 60/60 raw rubric points. A judge determines the actual score, so never describe full marks as guaranteed. Build and document enough verifiable evidence to satisfy every exceptional-level descriptor.

## Before building

1. Confirm the selected track: Technical or Business/Non-profit (called Organizational on Devpost).
2. Define the exact problem, intended users, primary SDG and target numbers, measurable baseline, and at least three target metrics.
3. Keep all work original and created during August 7-9, 2026. Record any open-source libraries, templates, public APIs, datasets, and external assets used.

## Requirements for every change

- Tie the change to one or more rows in `docs/judging-scorecard.md`.
- Prefer measurable outcomes over unsupported claims. Save citations, test outputs, benchmarks, survey/interview notes, and assumptions.
- Keep the demo path reliable and visually clear. Handle likely errors and empty/loading states.
- Record meaningful AI assistance in `docs/ai-use-log.md`, including tool, purpose, representative detailed prompt, output used, rationale, validation, measurable outcome, and boundaries where AI was not used.
- Never fabricate user feedback, metrics, research, partnerships, costs, or performance data. Label estimates and projections clearly.
- Maintain a polished pitch-ready explanation of problem, solution, evidence, SDG contribution, feasibility/execution, scalability, and next steps.

## Completion gate

Do not call the project submission-ready until all applicable evidence cells in `docs/judging-scorecard.md` are linked to real artifacts and the following are complete:

- Clear project description and explicit track selection.
- Pitch deck.
- Technical: working prototype plus repository or clickable mockup. Organizational: Business Model Canvas or structured organizational blueprint.
- Public or accessible 2-3 minute YouTube/Vimeo demonstration that introduces the team and shows/explains the solution.
- Smooth, timed demo; all team members contribute if the team has multiple members.
- Google Form submission from the official WhatsApp community completed before the deadline. Do not rely on a Devpost project submission; the live rules say Devpost submissions will not be reviewed.
- Eligibility, originality, attribution, code-of-conduct, and single-submission checks passed.

## Final audit

Score conservatively. Award an internal 10 only when the exact exceptional descriptor has documented evidence. Treat missing evidence as missing points, even if the feature exists.
